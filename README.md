Weekly project design journal for CART 470 Capstone Project.

#Week 2 Journal
So, we still don't know what we're doing. The meeting is soon, and that's okay that we don't know what we're doing yet.

I'm really interested in trying to engage with decolonial principles for this project, since we're working with AbTec, and those being foundational principles that possibly rearrange even the basic assumptions we have about accessing space.

For instance - we typically think of digital space as infinite, and constantly accessible. But how much of this is based in a colonizer mindset? That we can "use" whatever space we have, whenever we want to, in whatever ways we want? These digital spaces are tied to physical resources - water, land - where servers are stored, and how energy is used to power them. We are reaching the end of the digital expense. What does seeking permission to enter a digital space look like, aside from signing away identity/submitting to a surveillance system? Can this be done with respect and trust between the owner of the digital space and the person seeking to access it?

I have a lot of anxiety around changing the existing gallery space into something /I/ think is better - that feels very aligned with the way previous white people have acted in regards to indigenous space. But also asking them permission or having them approve every single little thing would be exhausting and micro-managey. So what's the balance between white guilt(?) and autonomy? 

I'm feeling somewhat lost as to what steps to take going forward, with this group of people I haven't worked with before. I'm also on my last semester and feel like I've already yeeted my brain outside of the university. But I am very excited about this project, and getting to explore interactions within this digital space. It's hard to think of technical restraints or concepts when we don't even know what platform we're going to be creating this in!!
